EBS · SF / CDMX — Virtual Exhibition Pavilion

EBBERKETT is the archive, documentation, and exhibition pavilion by @eloriabristastudio. We operate as the studio's experimental wing: an open space dedicated to exploring new graphic languages, visual arts, and hybrid ideas[cite: 21].

Today, tech tools allow us to achieve instant technical perfection. However, we choose to focus on two key factors: the purity of intuition and the stories behind the works[cite: 20]. We don't see technology as an enemy, but as a canvas; we are interested in exploring how software and human sensibility dialogue with each other[cite: 20].

More than a static gallery, this pavilion is a living archive and a collaborative ecosystem[cite: 20, 21]. An open platform to connect, incubate, and bring visibility to emerging and resident artists[cite: 21].
