EBS · SF / CDMX — Virtual Exhibition Pavilion

EBBERKETT is the archive, documentation, and exhibition pavilion by @eloriabristastudio. We operate as the studio's experimental wing: an open space dedicated to exploring new graphic languages, visual arts, and hybrid ideas.

Today, tech tools allow us to achieve instant technical perfection. However, we choose to focus on two key factors: the purity of intuition and the stories behind the works. We don't see technology as an enemy, but as a canvas; we are interested in exploring how software and human sensibility dialogue with each other.

More than a static gallery, this pavilion is a living archive and a collaborative ecosystem. An open platform to connect, incubate, and bring visibility to emerging and resident artists.
