Hi, I'm George.

Creative director, audio engineer, and designer focused on dynamic branding for musical and cultural projects. Merging modernist aesthetics with organic textures.

More info ↓

Eloria Brista Studio is a multidisciplinary boutique studio. In the environments where we work, public attention is ephemeral, which is why brands need to evolve constantly to remain relevant.

Our focus centers on creating dynamic identity systems. We design the essence and foundations of each project from scratch, structuring aesthetic variations that allow the identity to mutate and adapt across different eras or seasons, without losing its essence.

Whether through the development of a complete visual identity or through the integration of specialized audio production and sound design services (when the project requires it), we conceive each commission through rigorous research and experimentation.

We combine analog processes with digital precision, in a constant dialogue of trial and error where we document each stage of the workshop to ensure that your brand's universe is unique, relevant, and capable of transcending time.
